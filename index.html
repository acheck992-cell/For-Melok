01 · a simple thought

Some people make an ordinary day feel a little softer.

I think the world is full of tiny things worth noticing: the sky just before sunset, rain against a window, a quiet road, a song that finds the right words for you.

And sometimes, without planning it, you meet someone who makes you want to notice those things a little more.

“Not every meaningful thing needs to be loud.”

🌿

04 · places we dream about

There is so much world left to see.

Little streets in unfamiliar cities. Train windows and changing landscapes. Mountains disappearing into clouds. The sea at the end of a long journey. Cafés we find by accident.

I hope you get to visit the places you dream about, meet kind people along the way, and collect memories that still make you smile years later.

Somewhere green

A trail through trees, cool air, and no reason to hurry.

A city at night

Warm lights, a long walk, and a playlist for the ride home.

A place not planned

Because sometimes the best stories begin with “What if we go this way?”

05 · the human part

More than pretty words.

What I value most is something simple: being able to talk honestly, share random little things, laugh at silly stuff, and let a friendship grow at its own pace.

You don't have to be cheerful every day or have the perfect words. I hope the people around you make room for the real you — on your bright days and your difficult ones too.

I’m glad our paths crossed, Melok.

06 · a letter, just for you

Some words I wanted to leave here.

Tap the envelope whenever you're ready.

A little note, with no need to answer right away.

Hey Melok,

I made this little page because sometimes I find it easier to put my thoughts into words when I have a quiet moment. I wanted to make something personal for you — not something perfect, just something sincere.

I think you're a lovely person, and I'm happy that I got the chance to know you. I like the small things about getting to know someone: their stories, the places they love, the music they listen to, and the way they see the world.

And if I'm being honest, there's a little affection in my feelings too. I'm not saying this to ask anything from you or to make things complicated. I respect your life and your choices, and I don't want you to feel any pressure because of these words.

Whatever shape our connection takes, I genuinely hope life is kind to you. I hope you find beautiful places, people who understand you, reasons to laugh, and dreams that slowly become real.

Thank you for being someone I wanted to make this for. Take care of yourself, and keep a little space in your life for wonder.

— with warmth,
Bhaskar
